Republishing

02

03

Sharing a Link Is Welcome

You do not need permission simply to share a link to an original page on this website with friends, readers or through messaging and social platforms. Sharing the original website link helps readers reach the work in its intended form.

How to Request Permission

To make a permission request easier to understand, please include the following information in your email:

  1. Your name and, where relevant, the name of your organisation or publication.
  2. The title of the work you wish to use.
  3. How you intend to use it — for example publication, translation, narration or adaptation.
  4. Where it will appear — website, book, magazine, video, event or another medium.
  5. Whether the proposed use is commercial or non-commercial.

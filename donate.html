Support Education

Education can open doors that circumstances sometimes keep closed. This space is dedicated to supporting educational opportunities for students who may need a helping hand.

Education Initiative

Wish Educational Foundation

Wish Educational Foundation is the name associated with this educational support initiative. Its purpose is to encourage and support students in meeting meaningful educational needs and continuing their learning journey.

How Support Can Help

Educational needs can be simple, but even small assistance can make learning easier for a student.

01

Books & Learning Material

Helping students obtain useful books, reference material and other resources needed for their studies.

02

School Essentials

Supporting basic educational requirements such as notebooks, stationery and other learning essentials.

03

Educational Support

Providing appropriate assistance toward educational needs where support can help a student continue learning.
